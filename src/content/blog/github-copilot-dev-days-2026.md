I had the privilege of speaking at **GitHub Copilot Dev Days 2026** today, connecting with developers who are pushing the boundaries of AI-assisted development. Here are the key takeaways from my session.

## Session Highlights

This session focused on practical strategies for maximizing your productivity with GitHub Copilot, from everyday coding tasks to complex refactoring projects.

## Key Topics Covered

- **Effective prompting techniques**: Crafting prompts that get you precise, useful code suggestions
- **Integrating Copilot into your workflow**: Making Copilot a natural part of your development process
- **Advanced features**: Leveraging Copilot Chat, workspace agents, and custom instructions
- **Real-world examples**: Debugging, code review, and architectural decisions with AI assistance
- **Best practices**: Maintaining code quality while accelerating development

## Main Insights

The most impactful realization from today's discussions: developers who treat Copilot as a collaborative tool — providing context, asking follow-up questions, and iterating — get exponentially better results than those who treat it as a code generator.
